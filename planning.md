# Da Nang Family Trip Planbook

## Confirmed Trip Profile
- Destination: Da Nang, Vietnam
- Travel style: Family trip optimized for kids, heat management, short transfers, and flexible rest blocks
- Travelers:
  - Adults: 2
  - Child: elementary grade 2 girl
  - Child: 6-year-old boy
- Departure from Korea: 2026-07-24 Friday 23:00
- Return arrival in Korea: 2026-07-29 Wednesday 23:00
- Practical local schedule: 2026-07-25 Saturday to 2026-07-29 Wednesday
- Season note: Late July is hot and humid. Outdoor activities should be concentrated in the morning or after 16:00.

## Itinerary Scenario Ideas
- Scenario A / Ba Na Hills included:
  - Best when weather is clear and the family wants one iconic full-day activity.
  - Tradeoff: Expensive, crowded, and tiring.
- Scenario B / Resort-first:
  - Best when the goal is rest, pool time, beach, and low-stress meals.
  - Tradeoff: Fewer iconic attractions.
- Scenario C / Hoi An-focused:
  - Best when the family wants lanterns, evening walking, simple hands-on activities, and photos.
  - Tradeoff: Evening crowds and late return.
- Scenario D / Heat or rain backup:
  - Best when weather or kid energy makes outdoor sightseeing impractical.
  - Uses indoor meals, malls, cafes, museum, massage, and hotel rest.

## Family Scheduling Rules
- Keep one major activity per day.
- Avoid outdoor sightseeing between 12:00 and 15:30 where possible.
- Prefer GrabCar 7 or private car. Avoid motorbike transport with children.
- Build every day with a rest fallback.
- Keep dinner locations close to the current hotel unless the outing itself is the evening plan.
- For late-night plans, define a skip condition based on kids' energy.
- For each card or itinerary item, include:
  - Family fit
  - Kid-friendly point
  - Best time
  - Estimated duration
  - Estimated transfer from the current hotel
  - Heat or rain risk
  - Skip condition

## Initial Place Candidates

### Restaurants
- Pizza 4P's Da Nang
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Pizza and pasta are low-risk meals for children.
  - Caution: Popular, reservation recommended.
- Epice Restaurant
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Resort setting, high chairs, kid-friendly menu.
  - Caution: Mid-to-high price range.
- Chops Da Nang
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Burgers, fries, crayons, casual Western meal.
  - Caution: Better for lunch or early dinner.
- Madame Son
  - Family fit: Medium-high
  - Kid point: Cleaner Vietnamese meal option for family dining.
  - Caution: Confirm spice level.
- Fatfish
  - Family fit: Medium-high
  - Kid point: Riverside location and Western-friendly menu.
  - Caution: Pair with Dragon Bridge or riverside evening.
- Han Market snacks and fruit
  - Family fit: Medium
  - Kid point: Fruit and small snacks.
  - Caution: Crowded, not ideal for a long meal.

### Activities
- Ba Na Hills and Golden Bridge
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Cable car, Fantasy Park, cooler mountain air.
  - Caution: Full-day activity, weather and weekend crowds matter.
- Hoi An lantern evening
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Lantern streets, river walk, short boat ride.
  - Caution: Evening crowds and heat fatigue.
- My Khe / Non Nuoc beach time
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Sand play, light swimming, sunset walk.
  - Caution: Avoid midday sun.
- Nui Than Tai Hot Springs Park
  - Family fit: Medium-high
  - Kid point: Water park, hot springs, relaxed full-day alternative.
  - Caution: Private round-trip transport preferred.
- Dragon Bridge fire and water show
  - Family fit: High if kids are rested
  - Kid point: Short and memorable night show.
  - Caution: Starts at 21:00 Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and major holidays.
- Lantern-making or cooking class near Hoi An
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Hands-on activity, good alternative to passive sightseeing.
  - Caution: Confirm age suitability and session language.

### Markets And Shopping
- Han Market
  - Family fit: Medium
  - Kid point: Fruit, souvenirs, quick cultural stop.
  - Caution: Crowded and tiring; keep visit short.
- Lotte Mart Da Nang
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Air-conditioned shopping and snacks.
  - Caution: More practical than scenic.
- Hoi An Night Market
  - Family fit: Medium
  - Kid point: Lanterns, small souvenirs.
  - Caution: Crowded; avoid staying too late.

### Attractions
- Marble Mountains
  - Family fit: Medium
  - Kid point: Caves and short adventure feeling.
  - Caution: Many steps; morning only and keep it short.
- My Khe Beach
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Wide beach and sand play.
  - Caution: Strong sun in late July.
- Hoi An Ancient Town
  - Family fit: High
  - Kid point: Walkable evening area with lanterns.
  - Caution: Crowds and heat.
- Museum of Cham Sculpture
  - Family fit: Medium
  - Kid point: Indoor cultural stop.
  - Caution: Keep to 45 to 60 minutes.
- Da Nang Cathedral
  - Family fit: Medium
  - Kid point: Quick photo stop near city route.
  - Caution: Not a standalone family destination.
- Son Tra Peninsula / Linh Ung Pagoda
  - Family fit: Medium
  - Kid point: Big statue and sea views.
  - Caution: Needs car transfer and heat planning.

- New Orient Hotel to Hyatt:
  - Recommended: GrabCar 7 or hotel-arranged car
- Hyatt to Hoi An:
  - Recommended: Private round-trip car
- Hyatt to Ba Na Hills or Nui Than Tai:
  - Recommended: Private car with return pickup
- Hyatt to Happy Day Hotel:
  - Recommended: GrabCar 7
- Happy Day Hotel to airport:
  - Recommended: GrabCar or hotel-arranged car

## Weather And Risk Notes
- Late July is hot and humid.
- Dry season is generally favorable for beach and outdoor plans, but sudden rain is still possible.
- Rain backups:
  - Lotte Mart
  - Museum of Cham Sculpture
  - Cafe or kids cafe
  - Massage with family waiting plan
  - Hotel rest
- Heat backups:
  - Resort pool outside peak sun
  - Indoor lunch
  - Nap block
  - Short taxi hops instead of walking
